    FAQ

    Before you book

    Is this a real appointment?

    No. Sable Room is a front-end demo. Visits stay in local storage and are never sent to a salon.

    Are the prices charged?

    Menu prices are sample numbers. There is no checkout.

    Can I request a stylist?

    Yes, in the booking form. “First available” is a label only. A live book would check that stylist’s real calendar.

    What is the cancellation story?

    The sample policy is 24 hours. This page does not send reminders or charge a fee.
