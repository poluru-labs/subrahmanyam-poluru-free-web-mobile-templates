Services

Care that fits your pet’s day—and yours

From routine wellness to urgent concerns, choose how you want to be seen. Transparent visit types and prep instructions before you arrive.

Wellness exams

Annual and puppy/kitten schedules, dental checks, and nutrition consults with clear next steps.

Urgent care

Same-day slots for limping, vomiting, or minor injuries—triage online so the team is ready.

Telehealth

Video consults for behavior questions, post-op checks, and deciding if an in-person visit is needed.

Vaccinations

Core and lifestyle vaccines tracked in your pet profile with digital certificates when you travel.

Grooming & hygiene

Partner groomers and nail trims booked alongside vet visits at participating locations.

Senior care

Pain management plans, lab monitoring, and gentle handling notes shared with every provider.

Pet records

One health timeline for every paw, claw, and whisker

Harbor Paws keeps vaccines, prescriptions, and visit summaries in one secure place—easy to share when you switch clinics or travel.

  • Smart reminders

    Medication, flea/tick, and vaccine alerts by email or push—snooze when life gets busy.

  • Family access

    Invite partners or dog walkers to view schedules without full account control.

  • Private by default

    Records are encrypted and only shared with clinics you explicitly authorize.

How it works

Book care in three straightforward steps

01

Add your pets
