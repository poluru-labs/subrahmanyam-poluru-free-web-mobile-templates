Features

Built for civic conversation, not clicks

Everything is scoped to your area. Moderation tools and clear boards keep discussions useful and respectful.

Verified neighbors

Join with address or invite code. Reduce spam and keep conversations among people who live or work nearby.

Topic boards

Safety, events, housing, lost & found, and local politics—each with its own norms and volunteer moderators.

Smart alerts

Follow boards or keywords. Get email or push digests—never miss a boil-water notice or block-party flyer.

Events calendar

RSVP to meetings and festivals. Export to your calendar and share public links with non-members.

Searchable archive

Find past threads about permits, contractors, and school boundaries—years of local knowledge in one place.

Helpful votes

Surface the best answers in recommendation threads without turning every post into a popularity contest.

How it works

From signup to your first helpful reply

01

Claim your area

Enter your ZIP or accept an invite from a block captain. We match you to the right TownSquare.

02

Verify

Confirm with a postcard code or partner login. Read the community guidelines— they’re short and practical.

03

Participate

Post, reply, and vote. Follow boards you care about and mute the ones you don’t.

Moderation

Healthy forums need clear rules

Volunteer moderators and automated safeguards work together. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours on weekdays.

  • Be specific—location and dates help everyone
  • No hate speech, harassment, or doxxing
  • Label political advocacy and commercial posts
  • De-escalate; contact mods instead of pile-ons
