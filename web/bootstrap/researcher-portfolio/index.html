About

Research with clarity and consequence

My work sits at the intersection of human–computer interaction, machine learning evaluation, and responsible product practice.

Across academia and industry partnerships, I lead studies on how experts and everyday users interpret model outputs, recover from AI errors, and make high-stakes decisions with assistance systems.

Before joining ResearcherHub Lab, I contributed to applied research programs with product teams at scale and mentored graduate researchers building measurement frameworks for reliability, fairness, and usability.

I publish in venues such as CHI, CSCW, FAccT, and TOCHI, and regularly advise startups, NGOs, and public agencies on evaluation protocols that hold up outside the lab.
