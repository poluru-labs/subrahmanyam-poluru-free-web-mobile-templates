Judging workflow

From nomination to winner selection

A clear five-stage process designed for fairness, speed, and accountability.

  1. 01

    Nomination

    Applicants or nominators submit structured entries with evidence and endorsements.

  2. 02

    Screening

    Eligibility rules, completeness checks, and conflict-of-interest flags are applied.

  3. 03

    Expert Review

    Independent reviewers score against a published rubric with written rationale.

  4. 04

    Shortlist

    Top-ranked nominations advance to panel deliberation and finalist confirmation.

  5. 05

    Final Selection

    Winners are selected, notified, and published with transparent category outcomes.
