Process

A clear path from first call to install day

We keep decisions structured and communication steady so you always know what happens next.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We learn how you use each room, gather inspiration, and define scope, schedule, and investment range.

  2. 02

    Concept & layout

    Floor plans, material palettes, and 3D visuals align the direction before anything is ordered.

  3. 03

    Documentation

    Detailed specs for contractors, lighting plans, and FF&E schedules keep builds on track.

  4. 04

    Install & styling

    We coordinate deliveries, site visits, and the final layer of art and accessories.

About the studio

Design rooted in restraint and warmth

Atelier Form is a small team of designers and project leads based in Chicago. We believe interiors should age well—fewer trends, more texture, light, and pieces with story.

Every engagement includes a dedicated lead designer and transparent milestones, whether you are refreshing one room or opening a new location.

  • NCIDQ-certified leadership
  • Trade partnerships nationwide
  • Remote and on-site options
