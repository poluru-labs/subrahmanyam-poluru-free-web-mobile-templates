    FAQ

    Before you drop the car off

    Is this a real appointment?

    No. Bayline is a front-end demo. Visits stay in local storage and are never sent to a shop.

    Are the prices charged?

    Starting prices are sample numbers for the cards. There is no checkout.

    Do you take every make?

    The story covers common passenger cars and light trucks. A live shop would confirm diesel, EV, or exotic work by phone.

    Can I wait on site?

    Oil, batteries, and rotations are written as wait jobs. Brakes and diagnostics are drop-off in this sample.
