The stay

A small resort that still feels like a house on the gulf.

Maya Poluru rebuilt an old motor court into 48 rooms, a spa, and a dining room that looks at the dunes.

Mornings start with coffee on the boardwalk. Afternoons are the saltwater pool or Kavya Poluru’s spa. Evenings, Arjun Poluru’s kitchen plates Gulf catch and a short wine list.

Anika Poluru runs the desk. She will hold a late check-in, a crib, or a table at dusk if you tell her when you book.
