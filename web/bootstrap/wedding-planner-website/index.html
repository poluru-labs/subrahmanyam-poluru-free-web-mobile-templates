Services

Support at every level of involvement

From first venue tour to last dance, choose how hands-on you want your planner to be—we adapt to your style and schedule.

Full planning

Concept, budget, vendor search, contracts, and design meetings—we quarterback the entire journey with you.

Partial planning

You book key vendors; we refine timelines, review agreements, and fill gaps before the final month.

Day-of coordination

We take your plan and run the wedding day—vendor cues, setup checks, and calm problem-solving behind the scenes.

Guest experience

RSVP tracking, seating tools, shuttle timing, and accessibility notes so every guest feels considered.

Budget stewardship

Category caps, payment reminders, and transparent reporting—no surprise overages in the final week.

Destination & multi-day

Welcome events, rehearsal logistics, and local vendor liaisons for celebrations away from home.
