Preparation

What to bring to a first meeting

A timeline, the contract or order you already have, and the date you cannot miss. Leave the binder. Bring the question.

Read the brief

Write the story in one page: who, what changed, and what you want. Counsel can ask for the rest. A working firm would also send a conflicts list before the meeting is confirmed.

Fees

How fees are usually explained

Hourly, flat, or phased. The useful version names the work included, the work that costs extra, and when you will see an invoice.

Read the brief

Ask for the scope in writing before anyone drafts. A sample firm on this page would rather decline a matter than hide the meter. Figures elsewhere on the site are illustrative.

Transactions

Documents that keep a deal moving

A letter of intent, a disclosure list, and a closing checklist. Most delays start when one of those three is missing.

Read the brief

Put the economic terms in the letter before the long agreement. Name who owns the drafts. Set a date for the wires. The rest of the paper should serve those three decisions.
