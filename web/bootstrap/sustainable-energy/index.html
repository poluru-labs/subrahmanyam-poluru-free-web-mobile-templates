Services

Four parts of a calmer house.

Each service is described so a real studio can replace the copy with its own scope, warranty, and tariff.

Rooftop solar

Panels sized from the bill and the roof, with shade called out before anyone orders hardware.

Home storage

A battery for the evening peak, so the sunny hours are still useful after dark.

Heat pump

Heating and cooling planned with the electric bill, not as a separate surprise.

Monitoring

A monthly note: what the roof made, what the house used, and what still came from the grid.

Estimate

Start from the bill you already pay.

Enter a monthly amount and a home type. The sample formula is: yearly bill, times an offset for the home, times 0.75. Panel count is that offset energy divided by 420. Payback uses $310 a panel.
