Our story

Cooked for the people who come back.

Laurel began as a Sunday supper in Maya Poluru’s kitchen. It is still a small room, a short menu, and a long conversation.

We source from farms east of Austin and boats that call in the morning. The fire is oak. The wine list is short on purpose — Arjun Poluru pours what he would drink with the trout.

Reservations open 21 days out. We keep a few bar stools for neighbors. If you are celebrating, tell Anika Poluru when you book — she will set the table accordingly.
