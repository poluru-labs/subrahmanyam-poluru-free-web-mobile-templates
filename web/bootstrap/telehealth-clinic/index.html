Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers to common questions regarding telehealth appointments, insurance copays, prescriptions, and technology.

Our board-certified physicians can diagnose and treat the vast majority of non-emergency conditions including cold, flu, COVID-19 symptoms, sinus infections, seasonal allergies, bronchitis, sore throats, pink eye, urinary tract infections, skin rashes, pediatric ailments, chronic hypertension monitoring, and mental health counseling.

If your doctor determines medication is medically appropriate, an electronic prescription (eRx) is routed immediately through our secure SureScripts gateway to your preferred local pharmacy (CVS, Walgreens, Costco, Walmart, or independent pharmacy). Most pharmacies have it ready for pick-up within 15 to 30 minutes. Note: We do not prescribe DEA Schedule II controlled substances online.

Yes. PoluruCare is in-network with most major insurance providers including Aetna, BlueCross BlueShield, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Medicare Part B, and Humana. Most insured patients pay between $0 and $15 in copayments. If you are uninsured, our transparent self-pay visit rates start at just $49.

No downloads or software installs are necessary! Our HIPAA-compliant telehealth rooms run directly in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox) on your iPhone, Android, tablet, or desktop computer. You will receive an SMS and email link to enter the virtual waiting room with a single click.

PoluruCare is fully compliant with federal HIPAA and HITECH privacy standards. All video and audio streams are end-to-end encrypted using 256-bit AES encryption. Video consults are never recorded without explicit written consent, and electronic health records are stored in SOC-2 certified clinical vaults.

Telehealth is intended for non-emergency conditions. If you are experiencing chest pain, severe shortness of breath, signs of stroke (facial drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty), severe uncontrolled bleeding, or suicidal thoughts, please dial 911 immediately or go to the nearest hospital emergency room.
