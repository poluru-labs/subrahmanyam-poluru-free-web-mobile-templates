    FAQ

    Before you submit

    Is this connected to a real product?

    No. Signaldesk is a front-end demo. Votes, bookmarks, and submissions stay in local storage.

    Can I vote more than once?

    In this demo, one vote per idea per browser. Refreshing does not reset unless you clear storage.

    Do submissions email the team?

    Nothing is emailed or posted to an API. The form appends to your local list only.

    Are vote counts real?

    Base numbers are sample content. Your demo vote adds one locally to the displayed total.
