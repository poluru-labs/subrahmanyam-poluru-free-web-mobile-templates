FAQ

Before you hit play

Is Reeldesk a live streaming platform?

No. This is a front-end demo. Saved courses and enroll requests stay in local storage.

Can I watch videos here?

There is no video player, DRM, or CDN. Thumbnails are from Unsplash for layout.

What does Saved only do?

It filters the grid to courses you bookmarked in this browser.

Are prices real?

Dollar amounts are fictional sample labels. No checkout or cart runs on this page.
