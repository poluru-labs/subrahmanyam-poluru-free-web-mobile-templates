    Is Folio connected to real employers?

    No. The companies, dates, and stages are a fictional sample by Subrahmanyam Poluru.

    Where do added roles go?

    They stay in this browser. Restore sample clears them. Nothing is emailed or stored on a server.

    What does Move forward do?

    It steps a card from Saved to Applied, Interview, then Offer. Pass moves it off the open board.
