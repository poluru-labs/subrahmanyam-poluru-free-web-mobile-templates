    FAQ

    Before you cook

    Is this a live recipe app?

    No. Simmerbox is a front-end demo. Saved recipes and tips stay in local storage.

    Can I upload photos?

    Images are from Unsplash for the layout. There is no upload or account system.

    Where do hearts go?

    They are stored in this browser. Toggle Saved only beside search to see your list.

    Are nutrition facts included?

    Tags like vegetarian are story labels only. There is no nutrition API.
