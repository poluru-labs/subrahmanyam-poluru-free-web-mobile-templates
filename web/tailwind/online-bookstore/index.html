FAQ

Common questions

Is this a real bookstore?

No. BookHarbor is a front-end template with sample titles and demo cart behavior.

Where is my cart stored?

Only in your browser’s localStorage. Clearing site data resets counts.

Can I use the design commercially?

Yes — free for personal and commercial use. Credit to Subrahmanyam Poluru is appreciated.
