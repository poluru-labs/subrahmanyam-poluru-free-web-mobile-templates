    FAQ

    Before you wire this up

    Is this a live property system?

    No. Parkline is a front-end demo. Tickets and watchlist items stay in local storage.

    Can tenants pay rent here?

    The ledger table is sample data. There is no payment processor or bank link.

    What does the watchlist do?

    Bookmark icons save buildings in this browser. Use Saved only beside search to filter.

    Are the metrics real time?

    Numbers and bars are static samples for the layout. They do not pull from an API.
