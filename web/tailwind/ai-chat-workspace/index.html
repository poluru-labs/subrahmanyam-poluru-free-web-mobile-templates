FAQ

Common questions

Do you train on our data?
No. Threadline does not use customer workspace content to train models. Enterprise plans include zero-retention options with supported providers.
Can we use our own API keys?
Yes. Pro and Enterprise let you connect provider keys so usage bills directly to your account.
Is there a desktop app?
Threadline runs in the browser today. macOS and Windows apps are in beta for Pro teams.
