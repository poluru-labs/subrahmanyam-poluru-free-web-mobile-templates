How it works

Three calm steps

The real shelter would confirm visits by email. Here, the request is stored only in this browser.

  1. 1

    Choose a companion

    Read age, size, and temperament. Save a short list before you decide.

  2. 2

    Send a meet request

    Tell us your home type. A coordinator would reply with open visit times.

  3. 3

    Visit, then decide

    Meet in person at East Shelter. Adoption paperwork stays with the shelter team.

Meet request

Tell us who you want to meet

Pick a companion and leave a note about your home. Submitting saves a demo record on this device. Nothing is emailed and no fee is charged.

  • Same-week visits when the shelter calendar is open
  • Sample fees cover vaccines and a starter kit in the story only
  • You can remove a saved request at any time

    FAQ

    Before you visit

    Is this a real shelter application?

    No. Pawlane is a front-end demo. Meet requests stay in local storage and are never sent to a shelter.

    Are the adoption fees charged?

    Fees on the cards are sample numbers for the layout. There is no payment form.

    Can I adopt if I rent?

    Apartments are a home option in the demo form. A live shelter would ask for your lease pet policy.

    Where do saved pets go?

    Hearts are stored in this browser. Use Saved in the header or next to search to see only those companions.
