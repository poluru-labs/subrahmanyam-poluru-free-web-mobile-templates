    Guidelines

    Three rules for the demo board

    • Assume good intent. Ask clarifying questions before you disagree.
    • Keep it local. City-wide politics belong elsewhere; block-level detail belongs here.
    • Protect privacy. No doxxing, gate codes, or live location trails in sample posts.

    FAQ

    Before you post

    Is this a live forum?

    No. Commonroom is a front-end demo. Bookmarks and drafts stay in local storage.

    Can I create an account?

    There is no sign-up, OAuth, or backend. Names in forms are sample fields only.

    Where do bookmarks go?

    They are stored in this browser. Toggle Saved only beside search to filter your list.

    Are moderators real?

    Names and roles are fictional sample content for the template layout.
